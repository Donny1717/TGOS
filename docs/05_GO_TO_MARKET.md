# Go-To-Market

**Version:** 2026-09-06. Rewritten. The August GTM plan and the outreach list built from it both contained defects serious enough that reissuing them would have damaged the company's standing with the exact audience it was aimed at.

---

## 1. What was wrong, briefly

The outreach material referenced **PAS 91**, a standard withdrawn in April 2023, and **PPN 003**, which does not govern payment. It also named a person and job title at the UK's largest bid consultancy group that could not be found in any source, and misdescribed a senior charity executive as a freelancer.

A product whose entire pitch is "no unevidenced claims" cannot lead with unevidenced claims. Every fact in customer-facing material is now checked before it ships.

## 2. Sequencing

The August plan led with consultancies. That is backwards. Consultancies bill for bid writing by the hour; a tool that shortens evidence assembly reduces their billable time, and the largest of them already ships bid software.

**Correct order:**

1. **Independent bid writers.** Decide alone, buy at £149 without procurement, talk to each other. Fastest signal and the best early feedback loop. Build this list from the APMP UK member directory and its freelancer focus group. Target 15 names.
2. **In-house bid teams at SME contractors.** They carry the cost and capture none of the revenue. Target the Bid Manager, not the Commercial Director — the Director buys after the Manager asks.
3. **Community and standards.** APMP UK focus groups. Not a sales channel. See section 4.
4. **Consultancies.** Partner motion, different message. See section 5.

## 3. Message discipline

Before any send, confirm these are true of the shipped product on that day:

- Final export is genuinely blocked on unresolved evidence gaps
- The regulatory triggers named in the message are actually detected from an imported ITT
- The free plan exists and requires no card

If any is roadmap rather than reality, change the tense to "building". A provenance product caught overstating itself does not recover.

Do not write "most bid teams I speak to" until enough have been spoken to. Do not repeat a target's own published win-rate back at them as fact. Every named person and title is verified on their live profile before the message is written.

Length: a LinkedIn connection note caps at 300 characters. An opening message runs about 90 words. The August draft ran 250.

## 4. Community: ask for critique, not a meeting

APMP UK focus group leads are volunteers who run professional-standards work. They receive demo requests constantly and ignore them. They receive very few requests for professional judgement.

The credible opening is: *"I've built this on a hard constraint. I don't know whether it's a safeguard or an obstacle in real bid work. Tell me which — including if the answer is that it wouldn't survive contact with a live deadline."*

Ask questions that can be answered *no*. "Would this be useful?" gets a polite yes and teaches nothing.

**Do not approach until the platform meets the founder's own standard.** This is a one-shot audience.

## 5. Consultancy positioning

Not the same pitch. Lead with their **unbilled** work: chasing clients for the ISO certificate, the CRP, the financial evidence they were sure they had sent. Doccute gives the client a structured place to hold evidence before it reaches the consultancy. The writing stays theirs. The chasing mostly stops.

## 6. Competitive position

Four bid-software companies sponsor APMP UK events: AutogenAI, Loopio, CV Partner, Xait. Thornton & Lowe, now part of Procurement Growth Group, ships tender management software of its own.

All are document-production tools — write faster, reuse content, collaborate. **None is built around UK procurement rule enforcement.** Loopio and Xait are international and not tied to UK law. AutogenAI generates text, which is the problem Doccute exists to constrain rather than a competing solution to it.

That gap is the position. It is only defensible while the regulatory dataset is genuinely maintained, which is why the validator and its expiry policy are a commercial asset and not merely engineering hygiene.

## 7. Compliance in outreach

UK B2B cold outreach runs on legitimate interest. Corporate role-based addresses only. Unsubscribe line and privacy notice link on every email. Log the lawful basis and source for each contact — this company sells an audit-trail product and must keep one.

Do not automate LinkedIn connection requests. A restricted account costs the whole channel.

## 8. Volume and tracking

Ten to fifteen manual sends per week. One row per target:

```
name | company | title as displayed | profile URL | date verified |
tier | date sent | reply | reason
```

Every reply including a no is logged with its reason. The reasons are the asset.
